    Straight answers.

    Are you really open 24 hours?

    Yes. Nights, weekends and holidays. When the heat goes out at 2am, call (347) 599-6791.

    Do you charge for estimates?

    Estimates on new installs and replacements are free. We’ll look at the house, size the system and give you the number before any work starts.

    Ductless or central air: which should I get?

    If you already have ductwork in good shape, central air or a ducted heat pump usually makes sense. No ducts, an older home, an addition or a finished basement? Ductless is often cleaner and cheaper to install. We’ll tell you which fits after we see it.

    What brands do you work on?

    Most major brands, including Mitsubishi Electric, Fujitsu, Daikin, Carrier, LG, Rheem, Trane and York. Mitsubishi and Fujitsu ductless systems are a big part of what we install.

    Do you do commercial work?

    Yes. Rooftop units, split systems and service for shops, offices and warehouses, including crane-set replacements.
